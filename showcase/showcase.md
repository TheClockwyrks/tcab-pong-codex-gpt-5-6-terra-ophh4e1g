# Carom

Carom turns a clean paddle duel into a compact game of angles. The first serve tears across a midnight table, and every return asks whether to send it straight back or tempt fate through the middle.

Paddle movement leaves its signature on the ball. Catch it while climbing or dropping and the shot curls away, making a familiar rally feel like a tiny game of billiards at full speed.

The amber blockers turn the open center into a bank-shot puzzle. A confident rally can skim a wall, bend around an obstacle, and arrive at the far paddle from an angle that feels just unfair enough.

Play alone against a responsive opponent or share the keyboard for a quick first-to-eleven duel.
